# CR292: Move the Invocation Mode to the Advanced Tab

**Status:** captured 2026-11-25; for a later release, after [CR291](cr291-move-the-prompt-cache-option-to-an-advanced-settings-tab.md)
**Driver:** Navigator
**Requested:** on 2026-11-25, in the assessment of CR291: "concordo em deixar o invocation mode para uma CR separada em outra release."

## Friction

Invocation mode (Models › Global defaults, "Mirror runtime Pi" or "Raw local Pi") is the most advanced
option in Settings: by its labels it decides whether Pi runs with the Mirror around it, and a person who
changes it unawares may lose what the app is for. It sits among the model and the thinking level, which
people change daily.

## What Would Change

The option moves to Settings › Advanced. Open questions, for the plan:

- It is saved today with the model and the thinking level by "Save global defaults" and restored by
  "Restore Nautilus defaults". In Advanced it would save on change, like the prompt cache option, and the
  restore must still cover it.
- What "Raw local Pi" does at runtime was read only from its label in the assessment; trace it first.
- Whether Diagnostics' "Current session invocation controls" is the same concern and moves too.
