# v0.4.0-alpha.10 - Personas in the Sidebar

**Date:** 2026-11-08

## Highlights

- **A Personas section** under the Journeys lists every persona the Mirror keeps, with its image
  or colour, its name and its description. A click opens the persona's screen (CR221).
- **Settings beside the version.** The ⚙ sits in the brand block, next to the button that
  collapses the sidebar (CR220).
- **New Journey is the first Tree tool,** and an empty sidebar offers to create the first Journey
  (CR220).
- **Still Intel only, and not signed.**

## Where We Started

Alpha.9 let you open a persona only from its name in a Conversation where it had spoken. A
persona that had not spoken yet could not be reached from the app. The sidebar's footer held
Settings and New Journey, and a second ＋ for personas would have sat beside the first.

## What Changed

- **The Personas section.** At the foot of the sidebar, closed by default, "▸ Personas" with the
  count; open or closed is remembered. Open, it takes about half the sidebar with its own scroll
  and a filter that matches name, id and description, accents and case aside. The row of the
  persona whose screen is open is marked; a name saved on that screen renames its row at once. If
  the Mirror cannot be read, the section says so and offers "Try again", and the Journeys keep
  working (CR221).
- **The compact sidebar** shows one ◇ with the count; a click expands the sidebar with the section
  open (CR221).
- **Settings** moved from the footer to the brand block, beside the collapse button; in the compact
  sidebar it stacks under the expand button (CR220).
- **New Journey** moved from the footer to the Tree tools, first in the row, with the same default
  parent. Collapse all and Expand all read Collapse and Expand; at the narrowest sidebar they keep
  only their icons, their full names in the tooltip (CR220).
- **An empty sidebar** says "No Journeys yet" and offers "Create a Journey" in every view. When
  every Journey is hidden as inactive, it says so and points at the Tree tools (CR220).
- **The footer is gone** (CR220).

## What Does Not Change

The same Mirror core (`mirror-mind@0.43.0`) and the same Pi as v0.4.0-alpha.9. Nothing in a home's
database changes. The list only reads the personas; editing works as in alpha.8. Your stored
preferences are kept, and the new section starts closed.

## Conscious Exclusions

- **New Journey shows only in the Tree view,** not in Recent or Pinned; switch to Tree, or use a
  Journey's context menu, to create one.
- **Personas cannot be created or deleted here** yet; that waits on the Mirror core (CR222, CR223).
- **No ⋯ menu on a persona's row** yet; its only item would be Open, which the click already does.
- **No signing, notarization or Apple Silicon build** yet.

## What We Learned

A place should be born where it will stay. The list could have lived in Settings for now and moved
later; putting it in the sidebar from the start means that when a click on a persona one day opens
a conversation with it, nothing the hand has learned has to change.

## Next Horizon

Creating and deleting a persona from the sidebar, once the Mirror core can do both (CR222, CR223),
and the older open work (RS027).
