# v0.4.0-alpha.19 - A Conversation that keeps its Journey

**Date:** 2026-11-17

## Highlights

- **The Core moves to `mirror-mind@0.44.1`.** It carries the correction of CR094: every
  Conversation Nautilus provisions is marked as the host's, and a Mirror load from a terminal no
  longer rewrites its Journey, retitles it or appends a reply into it.
- **Nothing changes on screen.** This alpha carries no interface work: the same app as alpha.18
  over a newer Core.
- **Still Intel-only and unsigned on macOS;** the Windows bundle is unsigned.

## Where We Started

On 2026-09-26 a Mirror Mode load started without a named session resolved the Journey from a global
default and wrote it over the Conversation of the most recently active session, which was
Nautilus's. The Conversation then belonged to another Journey, every later delivery was refused as
a Journey mismatch, and two turns were recorded in the wrong place. Nautilus contained the damage
(CR093) but could not stop its cause, which lived in the Core. CR094 held that debt until the Core
could take it.

## What Changed

- **The pin.** `mirror-host/pin.json` names `mirror-mind` 0.44.1, release `v0.44.1`, commit
  `d2c565cd`, with the tarball npm serves checked by its sha256 and sha512.
- **What the Core fixed (its CR216).** `ensureConversation` marks the Conversation's Journey as the
  host's. The guess of which session a load belongs to skips a marked session, and the load never
  moves that Journey and never writes an empty persona or Journey over one it did not resolve. A
  Conversation provisioned before this alpha gets the mark the next time Nautilus ensures it.
- **Also in 0.44.1.** `--help` after a command answers its usage and runs nothing (CR215), and the
  operating instructions bound a task's size and describe the persona routing (CR211, CR213). The
  app does not use them; the terminal does.

## What Does Not Change

The same Pi, the same Node and the same screens as alpha.18. No schema change and no migration: the
home stays at `018_drop_hook_injected`, and the host's request to the Core is unchanged.

## Conscious Exclusions

- **The protection depends on both sides.** The mark is written by the Core Nautilus embeds, but
  the load that used to move the Journey runs in the Navigator's terminal `mirror`. It protects only
  once that one is also on 0.44.1 (`mirror runtime update`). CR093's containment stays for the rest.
- **No audit trail of a Journey change,** which was CR094's third option and was left out in the Core.
- **No signing or notarization,** on macOS or Windows, and **no Apple Silicon build.**

## What We Learned

A debt held in a register while the Core was out of reach was not forgotten, but it did not shrink
either. It closed in one working day once the Core could take it through its own review and
release, and the Desktop's part was reduced to a pin. Holding a debt is not the same as keeping it
visible: the register could close only when its last open item had a home in the Core.

## Next Horizon

Back and forward shortcuts and clickable path segments in the Agentic Field wait for the Navigator
to ask for them. The settlement timings of alpha.17 and alpha.18 on a large Journey still decide
whether the half of CR157 left out is recaptured, and CV-011, an embedded Pi, waits for an
Explorer pass.
