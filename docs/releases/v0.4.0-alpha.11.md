# v0.4.0-alpha.11 - Personas Created and Deleted

**Date:** 2026-11-09

## Highlights

- **Create a persona** from the ＋ beside "Personas", with its name, id, description, prompt and
  routing keywords (CR222).
- **Delete a persona** from its screen or from the ⋯ on its row, after a list of what goes, what
  is cleared and what stays as history. The Mirror takes a backup first (CR223).
- **"Image saved."** appears where a persona's image was chosen, so a Save button that stays off
  no longer reads as an error (CR224).
- **The Mirror core moves to `mirror-mind@0.44.0`,** which can create and delete a persona
  (Core CR207).
- **Still Intel only, and not signed.**

## Where We Started

Alpha.10 listed every persona in the sidebar and opened each one, but a persona could only be
made or removed in the terminal. On a persona's screen, an image was kept the moment it was
chosen while Save stayed off, and nothing near the image said so.

## What Changed

- **New persona.** With the Personas section open, its ＋ opens an empty screen. The id is
  proposed from the name (accents dropped, spaces as hyphens) and follows it until you type one;
  once the persona exists, the id cannot change. The Mirror's refusals, such as an id already
  taken, read as sentences. Created, the persona is listed at once and its screen becomes the
  edit screen, where an image can be chosen (CR222).
- **Delete persona.** "Delete persona…" on the persona's screen, or on the new ⋯ menu of its row
  (with Open), opens one confirmation (CR223). It lists:
  - what will be deleted: its definition, its generated descriptor, its integrations and its
    image on this device;
  - what will be cleared: the default persona of each Journey that names it, and the Mirror's
    remembered default persona;
  - what will be kept as history, under the persona's id: its Conversations, memories and runtime
    sessions. A bubble it signed keeps its name and colour.
- **A persona other personas inherit from** is refused, naming them.
- **Confirmed,** the Mirror writes a backup (`backups/pre-persona-deletion-…`) and deletes; the
  persona leaves the list. If it changed between the list and the confirmation, the deletion is
  refused and the list is read again (CR223).
- **A persona's image** shows "Image saved." under its buttons after a choice or a removal, until
  the next edit; the note says the image is saved apart from the Save below (CR224).

## What Does Not Change

The same Pi as v0.4.0-alpha.10. The new core adds calls and changes no table, so nothing in a
home's database changes until you create or delete a persona. Editing a persona works as before.

## Conscious Exclusions

- **No undo for a deletion** beyond the Mirror's backup file.
- **No image during creation;** it is chosen on the edit screen that follows.
- **A persona's id cannot be renamed.**
- **No signing, notarization or Apple Silicon build** yet.

## What We Learned

Two ways of saving on one screen need to be said where each one happens. The image was saved, but
the only proof was behind the screen.
