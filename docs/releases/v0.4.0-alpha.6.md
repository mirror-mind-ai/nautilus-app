# v0.4.0-alpha.6 - A Conversation Between Two

**Date:** 2026-11-07

## Highlights

- **Who speaks, beside what they said.** Each answer sits in a bubble beside the persona's avatar,
  a ◇ in a colour of its own, and its name. Your messages sit on the right, with your initial and
  the time. A turn the Mirror Core gave no persona is spoken by the agent, π, never by a name read
  from the model's own signature (CR213).
- **A finished turn folds.** While a turn runs its actions stay open above the answer; when it ends
  they fold into one line under it ("14 actions · Opus 5.5"). A turn that failed or was cancelled
  keeps its line open (CR212).
- **Corrections are yours.** A correction sent while the agent works is a smaller bubble of yours,
  before the answer it shaped, with what became of it beside your name (CR214).
- **No stray signatures, and a new conversation counts its turns** (CR211, CR210).
- **Still Intel only, and not signed.**

## Where We Started

Every turn was a card with a header naming the speaker, its actions listed above the answer long
after they mattered, and the copy button in the header. The Navigator's own messages were cards
too, and a correction sent mid-turn was a system box after the answer, where it read as a request
the answer had ignored. A persona's "◇ name" signature still leaked when the model signed at the
start of a second block. And one new conversation kept reading "Not started" after its first turn.

## What Changed

- **Speech, not cards.** One component draws both sides. Each bubble copies its own words, the
  button shown on hover or keyboard focus. Progress notes stay inside the bubble, quieter. A change
  of persona from the previous turn reads "before: <name>" (CR213).
- **A colour per persona.** Taken from the persona's id, and darkened on light themes until it reads
  at 4.5:1; the lilac name that failed on light themes is gone (CR213).
- **Persona names.** Until the Mirror Core hands over a persona's own name (Core CR203), the id is
  made readable: `product-designer` reads "Product Designer" (CR213).
- **No persona crosses as none.** A delivered context that names no persona, from the session or
  the live run, is recorded as such, so the agent speaks (CR213).
- **The turn's line.** Actions, surfaces and model, with the reading position anchored on the
  answer so nothing jumps when it folds. "Show turn details" is gone; latest and older turns read
  the same (CR212).
- **Corrections.** "↳ correction · ✓ reached the agent", "✓✓ applied", "✕ not applied": the same
  evidence as before, said beside your name (CR214).
- **Signatures.** A line that is only "◇ <persona>" is removed wherever it falls, except in code
  (CR211).
- **First turns.** A chapter index with a single unanchored Segment now covers the whole
  conversation, so its first turns are counted (CR210).

## What Does Not Change

The same Mirror core (`mirror-mind@0.40.0-next.1`) and the same Pi as v0.4.0-alpha.5. Nothing in a
home's database changes. Your stored preferences are kept.

## Conscious Exclusions

- **Persona colours can collide.** A colour hashed from 28 ids cannot keep every pair apart; the name
  always tells them apart. Whether to separate them per conversation is a later decision.
- **Files touched and duration** are not in the turn's line; the runtime does not keep them yet.
- **Persona names from the Core** wait for Core CR203.
- **The deletion dialog's pale list and title (CR205)** wait for a later version.
- **No signing, notarization or Apple Silicon build** yet.

## What We Learned

A colour attached to the whole turn painted whoever spoke inside it, including the Navigator; style
belongs to the speaker, not to the container. And a grid with no declared column grows to its
widest line: a code block in a progress note pushed the text past the bubble until every track was
bounded.

## Next Horizon

over persona names (Core CR203). Then CR205 and the older open work (RS027).
